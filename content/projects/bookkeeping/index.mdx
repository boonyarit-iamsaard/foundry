## Overview

**Bookkeeping** is a personal ledger for household money. It records what came in, what went out, and where the money sits now. I enter every transaction by hand, usually on my phone right after paying. There's no bank sync.

I used to keep household money in a spreadsheet, and the finance apps I tried in its place kept getting in the way. Most made me leave the entry screen just to add a category. So I built my own: a ledger I trust enough to keep, where balances match reality and corrections leave an honest history. I've kept my real ledger in it since September 2026. Sign-up is closed, because it holds my own household's money.

## What it does

- **Wallets** for cash, bank accounts and e-wallets. Each starts from a dated opening balance, and its balance on any date comes from its transactions.
- **Income and expenses** in exact Thai baht, to the satang, filed under two-level categories. A missing category can be created without leaving the entry screen.
- **Transfers** between my own wallets, recorded once and applied to both, and **refunds** against a specific expense.
- **Reports** for any month: income, net expenses, spending by category, a six-month trend, and every wallet's balance on a chosen date.
- **Corrections** that edit or delete a transaction in place, while a change history keeps what it looked like before.
- **An installable app** with light and dark appearance, built for one-handed entry and still comfortable for review on a desktop.

## How it's built

- **A single-page app and an API.** A Vite and TanStack Router client, installable as a PWA, calls a Hono API on Node.js. TypeScript throughout, in a pnpm and Turborepo monorepo.
- **PostgreSQL, Drizzle and Better Auth.** Data lives in PostgreSQL behind Drizzle ORM, and email and password sign-in uses Better Auth.
- **Tailwind CSS and shadcn.** The interface is built from shadcn components on Base UI.
- **One contract, generated types.** API routes describe their requests and responses with Zod schemas, the server generates an OpenAPI document from them, and the client's types are generated from that document.
- **Balances are derived, never stored.** A wallet's balance is its opening balance plus its transactions up to a date.
- **Exact money.** Amounts are stored and calculated in integer satang.
- **Backups:** a nightly job encrypts a database dump and stores it in AWS S3.
- **Hosting:** the app and its API run on Railway.
