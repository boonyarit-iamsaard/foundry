# Foundry

The personal site of Boonyarit Iamsa-ard: articles he writes and projects he builds.

## Language

"Status" alone is ambiguous: articles and projects each have their own, with unrelated values. Always say which one.

### Article status

**Article status**:
Whether an article is visible to readers.
_Avoid_: status (alone), state

**Draft**:
An article still being written; visible only while developing locally, never on the live site.
_Avoid_: unpublished, hidden

**Published**:
An article visible to everyone on the live site.
_Avoid_: live, public

### Project status

**Project status**:
Where a project is in its life, from first exploration to upkeep only.
_Avoid_: status (alone), stage, phase

**Experimental**:
A project in early exploration that may change a lot or be dropped.
_Avoid_: prototype, beta

**Active**:
A project still being built, with new features landing.
_Avoid_: in progress, ongoing

**Stable**:
A finished project that works as intended and may still gain a feature.
_Avoid_: done, complete

**Maintenance**:
A project kept running with updates and fixes only; it gains no new features.
_Avoid_: legacy, archived
